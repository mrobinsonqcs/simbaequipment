OUR MISSION

Simba Equipment was founded with one goal: to bring serious, heavy-duty machinery to job sites across the country at a price point that makes sense for contractors, utility companies, and industrial operations of all sizes.

WHO WE ARE

Based in Corpus Christi, TX, we supply a full line of equipment including telehandlers, man lifts, aerial work platforms, cranes, excavators, loaders, and concrete laser screeds. Every machine in our lineup is built to the same standard - heavy gauge construction, proven components, and performance that holds up under daily working conditions.

HOW WE WORK

We work directly with our manufacturer to maintain quality control and offer competitive pricing without cutting corners. Whether you need one machine or a full fleet, we handle the process from spec consultation to delivery.
